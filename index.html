Your Apple Health data, as files you own.

For you, a spreadsheet, or an AI agent you trust.

Your health data, in the open

Apple Health knows what you ate, how you slept and how your cycle went. Helsee takes that out into plain files you can keep, open in a spreadsheet, or hand to an AI assistant to analyse as time series.

Every export carries a README.md that explains each file and column, and a manifest.json with the same facts for machines. daily.csv has one row per day and one column per measure — energy_kcal, protein_g, sleep_h — so an agent can start working without guessing units.

Add a journal of what Health can’t measure — how you feel, symptoms, foods and events — and Helsee shows what tended to come before a better or a worse day.

What’s in an export

  • README.md — every file and column explained, for people and AI agents
  • manifest.json — time zone, period, metrics, units and counts
  • daily.csv — one row per day, Health and journal side by side
  • samples/<category>.csv — every sample, with its source app or device (JSON Lines too, if you like)
  • meals.csv — nutrition grouped into the meals you logged
  • journal.csv — your journal entries

An empty cell means nothing was recorded that day — never a zero.

Helsee reads Health only to make the files and insights you ask for. Your journal stays on your device, and exports go where you send them.

Helsee is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Insights show correlations in your own records.
